The OptimaLrn JLPT apps (N3, N4, N5) are designed to work offline. No account is required, and the developer does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Data we collect

None. The apps do not send any personal information or usage data to the developer or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

Your display name, language setting, lesson progress, XP, streak, daily goal, and review schedule are stored locally on this device only. Deleting an app deletes this data.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The apps contain no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information. Apple may process purchase information under Apple’s own privacy policy. The apps only check whether lifetime access is currently active.

Children

Because the apps collect no personal information, they collect none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
